For my great-grandfather

Clarence H. King

Born 1934

My great-grandfather Clarence is a sharp thinker, a generous teacher, and the reason I founded TNaxis in his honor.

CK

What he taught me

Clarence knows investing, and he's generous with what he knows. When we talk about it, he shares his strategies and on the off chance I can get a word in, he listens as I explain how I approach my own decisions. I value those conversations because I get to learn how he thinks and test my own thinking with someone whose judgment I respect.

Why I started TNaxis

When Clarence praised my knowledge and told me he saw great potential in me, it meant a lot coming from him. His encouragement stayed with me and made me realize I had a lot of potential to fulfill. I founded this company in his honor because I wanted him to know how much his belief in me matters.

Thank you, Grandpa, for sharing what you know and believing in what I could do.
